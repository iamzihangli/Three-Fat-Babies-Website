Tales of Three Fat Babies

Three rescues. Three very strong opinions. Straight from the source.

Pei6 yrs · the queen

I’m a six-year-old adopted girl who started my life in sunny Florida. Back then, I was a mama too — I had two litters of my own. Life was scary and full of unknowns, and I was so shy that Mama and Dad were really worried about me. Fast forward, and now I’m living my queen life!

After two years in Florida I moved to Alabama with Mama, where I reunited with Dad. Mama spoils me rotten, and honestly, I only love her (sorry, not sorry). I’m an expert food thief and love hiking with Mama, Dad and my brother Moo. Life is paws-itively amazing!

Moo4 yrs · moo moo cow

Hi, humans! I’m the chubbiest, cutest and most amazing boy you’ll ever meet. I’m four now. Mama adopted me as a baby in Florida (same as my sisi), and she says she picked me because my cow spots are hilarious. So now she calls me Moo Moo Cow… ALL. THE. TIME.

I love EVERYTHING. Chewing stuff, playing the dragging game, and most of all annoying my sisters. Seriously, it’s my best talent! Sometimes my mischief gets Mama and Dad all mad, but honestly? Eh, I don’t care.

Cata3 yrs · the only cat

Meow. I’m the only cat here, and I’m three years old. Life didn’t always look like this — I used to roam the streets near Dad’s old apartment. But one day I saw Mama and Dad, and I knew they were my humans. Just like that, I had a home!

Everything was strange at first. I had to share my space with a not-so-friendly sister dog and this huge, chunky, kind of scary dog. We didn’t hit it off right away. But now? We all live together peacefully. I’m snuggly (on my terms) — but don’t get too close unless I say so!
