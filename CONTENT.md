## Tales of Three Fat Babies

### Pei — 6 yrs · The Queen · Expert Food Thief · `assets/pei.jpg`
I’m a six-year-old adopted girl who started my life in sunny Florida. Back then, I was a mama
too—I had two litters of my own. But life was scary and full of unknowns, and I was so shy that
Mama and Dad were really worried about me. Fast forward, and now I’m living my *queen* life!

After two years in Florida, I moved to Alabama with Mama, where I reunited with Dad. Mama spoils
me rotten, and honestly, I only love her (sorry, not sorry). I’m an expert food thief and love
hiking adventures with Mama, Dad, and my brother Moo. Life is paws-itively amazing!

### Moo — 4 yrs · Moo Moo Cow · Professional Nuisance · `assets/moo.jpg`
Hi, humans! I’m the chubbiest, cutest, and most *amazing* boy you’ll ever meet. I’m four years
old now. Mama adopted me as a baby in Florida (same as my sisi), and she says she picked me
because my cow spots are hilarious. So now she calls me Moo Moo Cow… ALL. THE. TIME.

I love *EVERYTHING*. I love chewing stuff, playing the dragging game, and most of all, annoying
my sisters. Seriously, it’s my best talent! Sometimes my mischief gets Mama and Dad all mad, but
honestly? Eh, I don’t care.

### Cata — 3 yrs · The Only Cat · Snuggly On Her Terms · `assets/cata.jpg`
Meow. I’m the *only* cat in the house, and I’m three years old. Life didn’t always look like
this—I used to roam the streets near Dad’s old apartment. But one day, I saw Mama and Dad, and I
*knew* they were my humans. And just like that, I found myself with a home!

Everything was new and strange at first. I had to share my space with a not-so-friendly sister
dog and this huge, chunky, and kind of scary dog. Let’s just say we didn’t hit it off right away.
But now? We all live together peacefully.

I’m snuggly (on my terms, of course) and love my life, but let’s be clear—don’t get *too* close
unless I say so!
