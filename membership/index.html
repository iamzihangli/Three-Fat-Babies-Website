Who the member is

There are two names on every membership. Only one of them is the member.

The member

Your pet

The membership is in their name, the same name that goes in gold across the top of their frame.

It follows them and nobody else. It cannot be passed to another animal, and it does not end. Your pet will always have a place in our company.

The one who signs them up

My Human

That is you. It is what we call the person who pays for the membership, sends us the photos, and gives the portrait somewhere to sit.

You are who we write to, and who we look after if anything ever happens to the piece.

Why a warranty, of all things

Most of what we make is in memory of someone. If a piece like that gets knocked off a shelf, it is not an ornament that broke. So we decided the promise should be simple: whatever happens to it, we will put it right.

03

A few honest answers

Anything else, just ask.

Can the member be a pet who has passed away?

Yes. Most of them are. Send whatever photos you have and we will work from those. Their place with us is the same.

And a pet who is still here?

Of course. Plenty of people want their dog on the mantel while the dog is asleep under the table.

Why is my pet the member, and not me?

Because it is about them. The portrait is of them, the name on it is theirs, and the promise we make is to keep them looked after.

Can I give one as a gift?

Yes. If someone you know has lost a pet, this is a kinder thing to send than flowers. Their pet becomes the member and they become My Human. Tell us in your email and we will sort out the details.

Is it really just one payment?

Yes. We call it a membership because your pet stays with us, not because we keep charging you.

Hear about the launch first

We will email you once, when membership opens. Nothing else.
